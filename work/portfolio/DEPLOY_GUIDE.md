# 90-Second Netlify Deployment Guide ("Empty but Live")

Follow these steps to put your portfolio live right now on Netlify's free tier.

---

### Step 1: Open Netlify Drop
1. Open your browser and navigate to: **[app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)**
2. (If prompted, log in with your free Netlify account or sign up with GitHub/Email at [app.netlify.com/signup](https://app.netlify.com/signup)).

---

### Step 2: Drag the Portfolio Folder
1. Locate the folder:
   ```
   d:\Internships\Internship - FlyrankAI\Week 1\FlyRank-ml-starter\work\portfolio
   ```
2. Drag the **entire `portfolio` folder** directly into the upload area on Netlify Drop.
   > **Note:** Netlify will instantly detect `index.html` at the root and deploy your site in ~5 seconds.

---

### Step 3: Set Your Clean Site Name
1. Once deployed, click **Site configuration** (or **Site settings**).
2. Click **Change site name**.
3. Enter your custom name:
   ```
   abdullah-naeem-ml
   ```
4. Save it! Your permanent live address will be:
   👉 **`https://abdullah-naeem-ml.netlify.app`**

---

### Step 4: Verify on a Second Device (Your Phone)
1. Open the URL on your mobile phone:
   `https://abdullah-naeem-ml.netlify.app`
2. Confirm the page loads cleanly with the Plus Jakarta Sans typography, monogram `AN.`, and live green indicator dot.
3. Take a screenshot on your phone to keep as proof of the second-device verification.

---

1. Open your **Claude Project** for the portfolio.
2. In **Project Knowledge**, upload:
   - `work/claude_project_knowledge_bundle.md` (Contains everything consolidated: Style note, identity kit tokens, content map, and architecture rationale).
   - Or upload `work/fl01_identity_kit.md` and `work/fl01_content_map.md`.

